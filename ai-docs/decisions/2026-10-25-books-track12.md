# Books track 12 rulings: measure fixes from the image-processing and graphics books

Small design calls made while fixing the measures. The proposals came from reading notes on Gonzalez and Woods,
*Digital Image Processing* (4th ed.: §4.5 aliasing, §5.2-5.3 noise estimation, §10.3 Otsu's separability) and
Hughes et al., *Computer Graphics: Principles and Practice* (3rd ed.: §28.4 lightness, §5.4 and §28.19 surround).
The notes live outside this repository. Each "verified" claim was reproduced before the fix.

- **H1. Only `small()` changes; `resize_box` keeps its nearest pre-step.** `ahash` and `dhash` call `resize_box`
  directly, and changing it would change every perceptual hash and the dedupe distances tuned on them. The
  measuring copy (`small`, which every 256 px measure reads) now averages every source pixel: Pillow's BOX filter
  when Pillow is there, 2x2 halving otherwise, and only for reductions over 4x, so smaller reductions are unchanged
  byte for byte. Reproduced: 3000 px stripes of period 3 read a luma SD of 30.4 before, 1.3 after (Pillow BOX 1.3).
- **H2. Value is L*/100; the key cuts move to 0.38 and 0.68.** Those are the L* of the old luma cuts (0.35, 0.65)
  for neutral greys, so greys keep their key and only saturated colours move (pure blue 0.07 to 0.32). On 60 real
  AI stills, 2 changed key, both sitting on the line (0.37). `luma_mean` stays beside it. Spatial measures (edges,
  symmetry, balance, focal cell) still read luma; `setfit` keeps its own luma, since it gates relative to a series.
- **H3. Notan cuts at Otsu (two values) and k-means (three), on L*.** Shares and cuts are reported, so a high-key
  picture says 0.8 light instead of the median's fixed 0.5.
- **H4. Separation is a reading, not a flag.** `light` reports Otsu's eta as `separation`; under 0.5 (heuristic; the
  book's hard example scores 0.47) a reading says the light and shadow numbers are weak. 0 of 60 real stills fell
  under 0.5 (10th, 50th, 90th percentiles 0.67, 0.83, 0.95), so it fires only on genuinely one-family pictures.
- **H5. Grain by Immerkaer's estimator over the flattest tenth of a 16x16 grid.** The kernel cancels flat and
  linear tone, and restricting it to the lowest-Sobel cells keeps edges out. Readings at 1 and 3 L* units are
  heuristic: all 60 AI stills read clean (median 0.06), degrade noise at SD 8 reads 2.9, SD 16 reads 5.6. Known
  limit: a frame filled edge to edge with fine texture (a carpet photo read 5.7) has no flat cell, so texture reads as
  noise. Written into cli.md.
- **H6. comp subtracts 20 sigma squared from every cell before the sharpness ratio.** 20 is the sum of the 4-neighbour
  Laplacian's squared weights. On flat shapes the old ratio fell 999, 18, 3.7, 2.0 as grain rose (SD 0, 3, 8, 16 in
  RGB levels) and fired at 16; with the floor 999, 745, 38, 11, no flag. A uniformly textured image still fires.
  On 60 real stills the flag fired on 3 before and after, the same 3.
- **H7. The JPEG guard tests the 8 px block lattice, not luma.** The notes proposed confirming a 2 px lattice on
  luma alone; that failed on the reproduction, because the steps are 8x8 DCT block edges, visible in luma too, and
  every one of them is also on the 2 and 4 px lattices (97% of the smooth JPEG's steps sat on the 8 px lattice; real
  scale 2 art has about a quarter there). A found scale of 2 or 4 on a JPEG with 80% or more of its steps on the 8 px
  lattice becomes 1 with a note; a found 8 or 16 keeps its value with a note to look, since real scale 8 art and
  block edges cannot be told apart this way.
- **H8. `sheet` defaults to neutral grey (119, L* 50).** White makes colours read darker and duller beside it
  (simultaneous contrast); `--bg 255,255,255` gives the old sheet. Transparent cells composite on the same colour.
- **H9. Degrade `alias` shrinks by 2.7, not 3.** A whole factor reproduces block fixtures exactly (the test caught
  it); 2.7 always resamples.
- **H10. Not built from the notes:** corner doubles (track 13, with the pixel measures), `--edge-otsu` (only if a
  fixture shows the global split wrong; `--box` covers it), and the comfyui-gen and pixelwright items, which belong
  to other repositories.
