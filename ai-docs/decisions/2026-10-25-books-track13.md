# Books track 13 rulings: pixel-art measures and text

Small design calls made while building the pixel-art measures and rows proposed by the round 2 reading notes
(Azzi, *Pixel Logic*; Silber, *Pixel Art for Game Developers*; Dawe and Humphries, *Make Your Own Pixel Art*;
Hughes et al., *Computer Graphics: Principles and Practice*; Gonzalez and Woods for the corner rule). The notes
live outside this repository.

- **I1. The measures live in aw_measure.py**, not aw.py (83 KB of its 100 KB line); `pixel_one` calls them.
- **I2. Every new number is reported, never failed.** None has owner labels or hand-made pixel art behind it (none
  was available locally). The real batch was 30 AI renders reduced to 48 px with a box filter, quantised to 12
  colours by median cut and cleared of their border colour: banding 0 to 6.5 per 100 edge pixels (median 3.3), corner
  doubles 0 to 61, pillow on none (edge_corr mostly negative), interior jaggies 0 to 10. Banding overlaps the hugging
  ring fixture (4.7), so it is a locator with coordinates (L-019).
- **I3. Banding accepts the next row's run straight under or one pixel along.** The notes' exact-span rule found 0
  hits on a ring hugging a curved outline; the diagonal shift is Silber's super pixel.
- **I4. Corner doubles use a staircase condition.** A pixel with exactly one same-colour neighbour across and one up
  or down, the diagonal between them a different colour, and one arm turning back parallel to the other. Square
  corners (arms running on) are not counted; both pixels of a doubled stair step are. It is the same rule as
  pixelwright's `clean.corners`, so the count goes to 0 after that fixer.
- **I5. Pillow: rank correlation, 8 directions, lines 0.6 and 0.4** (heuristic). A disc shaded from the rim scored
  0.97/0.00, the same disc lit from the top left 0.36/0.68; the pillow degrade moves the lit disc to 0.97. Needs
  transparency and 100 opaque pixels.
- **I6. Interior jaggies skip outline colours** (half or more of their pixels on the silhouette) and colours under 5%
  of the opaque pixels; otherwise the outline's profile would repeat the silhouette count.
- **I7. Tile repetition lines at 6 L* (half against half) and a spread of 1.0;** a flat border side counts only
  when the tile's inner rows are mostly busy, so a plain tile does not fire. On 20 seamless tiles made from render
  crops (pixelwright `tile`), 4 fired; viewed in a 3x3 repeat the flagged ones stripe or lump and an unflagged one
  does not. On whole scenes it fires on most, as it should (they are not tiles).
- **I8. `--bg` loss line at 10 L* and 30% of the silhouette** (heuristic; the principle is Azzi and Dawe and
  Humphries). A dark-outlined disc is lost on #101010 and kept on #c0c0c0.
- **I9. Text kept to what the books add.** Vocabulary takes only the two prompt words (contact shadow, Fresnel
  reflection); Mach band and specular aliasing are critique words, left out as in track 6. The Roblox and Unity
  import rows belong to the engine plugins (round 2 track 19). The fractional shift, flame and ramp builder are
  pixelwright items.
